author: "Ziqi Zhang (ziqiz13)"

Suppose model A has a higher overall test AUC than model B, but model B has greater sensitivity in the range where specificity is at least 0.95. In a medical screening problem where false negatives are much more costly than false positives, which model should be preferred? Why does overall AUC not settle this decision, and what additional information or validation would be needed to choose an operating cutoff?
